sales and strategy · hyderabad, india · engineer turned strategist

i'm a sales and strategy person who started out as an engineer.

that order matters to me. before i ever sold anything, i built and fixed things in hyderabad, and i learned how a product actually works long before i learned how it's sold. that grounding still shapes how i think: get under the hood first, understand the machine, then work out how to take it to people.

from engineering i went to escp for a master's, split across paris and berlin. two countries, two languages, two ways of running a classroom and a company. then four months at dhl's headquarters in bonn, on corporate strategy, seeing how a global business steers itself from the centre.

after that came a rotation programme that moved me across hyderabad, singapore, gurgaon and manchester. key accounts in one place, headquarters strategy in another, and plenty of time in the field. i learned that selling looks different in every market, and that the fundamentals underneath it barely change at all.

most recently i spent three months with fieldwire in the uk, based in manchester, before coming back home to hyderabad. the thread running through all of it is a deliberate move: from hardware toward software, and from pure sales toward strategy.

right now i'm in hyderabad, learning german and working toward the goethe a2, and reading a guide to the good life. five countries lived in, twenty-one and counting travelled through, and a shelf of fifty-plus books i keep adding to. i speak english, telugu and german.

the why behind all of it is simple: i believe access to education and knowledge is how we solve the hard problems. that isn't an abstraction for me. i was once in someone else's shoes, and someone made room. so i take free calls, fifteen, twenty or thirty minutes, no fee. if a call was useful and you want to give something back, you can make an optional contribution toward a child's education, and i'll match it one for one. giving back is the point, not a side effect.

my writing lives at karthikonteddu.com, published under karthik onteddu. if you want the longer version of any of this, the essays, the field notes, the shelf, that's where it is.

the fastest way to reach me is email, and i'm also on linkedin.
